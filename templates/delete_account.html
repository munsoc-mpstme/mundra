Delete your Delego account

Delego is the MUNSociety MPSTME app for Mumbai MUN. You can delete your account and the data stored with it at any time, in the app or by email.

In the app

  1. Open Delego and log in.
  2. Go to your profile.
  3. Tap Delete account and confirm.

Your account is deleted straight away.

By email

If you can no longer log in, email {{ support_email }} from the address you registered with, with the subject "Delete my Delego account". We will delete it and reply to confirm.

What is deleted

  • Your login and password
  • Your profile: name, email, backup email, phone number, date of birth and gender
  • Your Mumbai MUN details: committee, country, diet and food notes
  • Your past MUN experience
  • Your meal-collection history and your food badge QR code
  • Any OC team memberships

Messages sent in a committee's break-coordination chat (used by organising committee members only) are kept so the conversation stays readable for the other people in it.
